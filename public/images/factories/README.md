## Quy tac anh

- Hien tai website dang dung `imagePath` lam anh dai dien cho moi nha may; vi vay upload `exterior.jpg` truoc.
- `production-line.jpg`, `product-stack.jpg`, `quality-control.jpg` va `video-poster.jpg` da duoc chuan hoa cho gallery va video poster. Chung se khong tu dong hien thi neu chua duoc gan vao giao dien.
- Uu tien anh that cua dung nha may, khong dung anh cua nha may khac.
- Dung JPG hoac WebP; nen nen anh con khoang 300-500 KB neu chat luong van tot.
- Anh ngang, anh sang ro, khong co watermark va khong chen so dien thoai/email.
- Can co quyen su dung anh cho website.
- Upload `exterior.jpg` truoc. Day la anh quan trong nhat cho giao dien hien tai.
- Ten file va ten thu muc phai viet thuong, dung dau gach ngang nhu mau tren.
